01 / Overview

Turning Olympic data into useful insight

This project explores how Olympic performance varies across countries, sports and regions. It brings together athlete information, medal outcomes and sports investment data to support meaningful comparisons.

⌁

The opportunity

Medal tables show who won. Deeper analysis helps explore where performance is strongest, how participation changes and which sports may deserve further attention.

◎

The approach

Prepare and validate data, define consistent metrics, build comparative visualizations and present the results through interactive Tableau dashboards.

02 / Problem statement

What drives sporting success?

Olympic performance can reflect athlete participation, sport specialisation, regional strengths and investment. The challenge is to examine these dimensions together without confusing correlation with causation.
